Meadow Evolution 3D

Creatures that find food reproduce, and their children inherit slightly changed speed and vision. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, pick a power and click the ground. 2D version

Orange creatures are well fed, pale ones are starving, bigger bodies have longer vision, red rings mark deaths.

About this project

An evolving ecosystem you can watch and play with in 3D. Creatures have two inherited traits, speed and vision. Those that find enough food reproduce, and their children inherit slightly mutated traits, so natural selection happens on screen.

What I found (preliminary, one run per setting): more food supports more creatures, and at very low food the population died out. When food was scarce, creatures tended to evolve longer vision (about 112, against about 81 in the abundant-food runs). Speed was capped in these runs, so it was not a fair test.

Built with JavaScript, Three.js and Chart.js.
